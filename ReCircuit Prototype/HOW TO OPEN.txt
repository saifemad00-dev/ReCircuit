ReCircuit prototype - how to open
=================================

1. Unzip the folder first (right-click > Extract All). Don't open it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).
3. Log in with a sample account:

   layla@campus.ac.ae   /  demo1234   (student)
   omar@campus.ac.ae    /  demo1234   (student)
   admin@campus.ac.ae   /  admin1234  (admin)

No internet or installation needed.
Data is saved in your own browser only. Click "Reset sample data" at the bottom to start again.
See README.md for the requirements each screen covers and the test cases.
